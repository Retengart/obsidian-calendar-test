## Purpose of the connection

Obsidian Calendar Test connects your own Google Calendar to desktop Obsidian through Google Calendar Tasks Sync 1.7.0. These terms cover the Calendar-only configuration described on the [home page](@/_index.md). Keep task mirroring disabled and do not grant Google Tasks access for this setup. Use the connection only for accounts and calendars you are authorized to manage.

The owner of this OAuth application provides the connection configuration, not Obsidian, Google Calendar or the third-party plugin itself. The plugin's license and distribution terms are available in its [source project](https://github.com/PubCyBerry/obsidian-google-cal-sync/tree/1.7.0). The terms of the Google and Obsidian services you use also apply.

## Changes to your calendar

Creating, editing and deleting events in the plugin can change data in Google Calendar. Check the selected calendar, date and time before saving. Test the setup with a separate test calendar before using it for important events.

Synchronization availability and speed depend on the plugin, your connection and Google's services. The connection does not promise instant delivery of changes, freedom from conflicts or authorization that lasts indefinitely. Google may require you to sign in again.

## Stopping use

You can stop using the connection and revoke access in your [Google account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). Revoking access does not automatically delete local data. The [Privacy Policy](@/privacy.md) explains local retention and removal, including the limits of the plugin's cleanup commands.

## Support

Send questions about setup and access to [calendar-plugin-obsidian-beta@googlegroups.com](mailto:calendar-plugin-obsidian-beta@googlegroups.com). Do not include secrets or personal calendar data.
